1. Your One-Run Master Plan

One Courier. One save. Everything.

You step out into the Mojave sun with a bullet scar in your skull, a Pip-Boy on your arm, and absolutely everything to do. This guide walks you through a single, Hardcore-compatible playthrough where your female Courier:

  • Experiences all main quests, side quests, and DLC questlines.
  • Triggers as many unmarked quests and personal stories as possible.
  • Discovers every location and collects all base-game snow globes.
  • Maxes all skills and pushes SPECIAL as high as the game reasonably allows.
  • Earns every achievement/trophy, including DLC and GRA, in one save.

The walkthrough is written to be kept open while you play. It tells you where to walk, who to talk to, and what to pick up, but avoids heavy plot spoilers.

Habit Before you leave any hub (town, Vault, major base), do a slow sweep: talk to every named NPC, check every terminal, and comb through shelves and desks for skill books and snow globes.

2. Character Creation & Hardcore Setup

Building the perfect female Courier.

2.1. Difficulty & Hardcore Mode

When Doc Mitchell asks about Hardcore Mode, choose it on and keep it on for the entire run. You can leave combat difficulty at Normal (or higher if you like), but do not toggle Hardcore off if you want the related achievement.

2.2. Starting SPECIAL (Before Implants)

Use this SPECIAL spread in Doc Mitchell's chair, before any implants:

  • Strength 5 – Good enough for most weapons; later boosted by DLC perks.
  • Perception 5 – Solid for accuracy and Perception checks.
  • Endurance 7 – Vital for more implants (one per END point + a DT implant).
  • Charisma 1 – Conversation power is in your skills, not this stat.
  • Intelligence 9 – You'll raise it to 10 immediately via implant.
  • Agility 7 – Great AP and reload speed, helpful for guns and sneaking.
  • Luck 6 – Boosts crit chance and skill points slightly; can be raised by implant.

2.3. Traits for a One-Run Completionist

  • Good Natured – More points into non-combat skills (Speech, Repair, etc.), which you want for checks and crafting.
  • Small Frame – +1 Agility at the cost of more fragile limbs. You'll stock doctor's bags anyway, so this is manageable.

If your game version offers the Skilled trait, it's also excellent for a long run: more skills in exchange for slightly slower leveling, which is no problem over 50 levels.

2.4. Early Perk Plan (Don't Improvise These)

Grab these perks at the earliest opportunity:

  • Level 2: Black Widow – Extra damage versus male targets and special dialogue options with several key men in the story.
  • Level 4: Educated – +2 skill points per level from now on. This is mandatory for a max-skill run.
  • Level 6: Comprehension – Doubles the benefit of skill books and improves magazines.

Other early perks to pick up soon after:

  • Cherchez La Femme – Fits your female Courier and gives bonuses against women plus extra dialogue.
  • Later, mix in combat perks (Guns, Toughness, Better Criticals) and quality-of-life perks (Jury Rigging, Sneak perks) as you like.

2.5. Faction & Ending Strategy

The NCR, Mr. House, Caesar's Legion, and Yes Man endings are mutually exclusive on a single branch, but you can see them all from one save with careful timing:

  • Progress each faction's main questline in parallel, stopping whenever one asks you to directly betray or destroy another major faction.
  • Just before the final Hoover Dam battle, create a special save named something like Pre-Hoover Dam – Final Choice.
  • Finish the game with one faction, watch the ending, then reload that save and make a different choice.

2.6. DLC Order for Story & Difficulty

Play the four story DLCs in this order, after you're mid-level but before Hoover Dam:

  1. Dead Money
  2. Honest Hearts
  3. Old World Blues
  4. Lonesome Road

This order makes the narrative references line up and ramps difficulty well, while giving you powerful perks and gear before the final battle.

3. Goodsprings – Waking Up in the Mojave

From Doc's house to your first snow globe.

3.1. Doc Mitchell’s House – Wake-up Loot Route

You come to on Doc Mitchell's couch in Goodsprings. Once you finish character creation, it's time to quietly strip his house of anything useful.

  1. After you stand up, walk through the house and loot methodically:
    • Medical room (where you did the Special test): on shelves and counters, pick up every Stimpak, RadAway, Rad-X, Doctor's Bag, and Med-X.
    • Kitchen: take all food and drink you see – canned goods, clean water, Sunset Sarsaparilla, and Nuka-Cola. These will keep Hardcore needs manageable early on.
    • Office/workbench room: grab Scrap Metal, Scrap Electronics, Wrenches, Duct Tape, and Wonderglue. These are your future Weapon Repair Kit components.
    • Bedrooms: loot dressers and cabinets for clothing, light armor, caps, and any chems you see.
  2. Accept the gear Doc gives you (9mm pistol, Vault 21 jumpsuit, ammo) and agree to Hardcore Mode.
  3. Before leaving, use his sink, fridge, and bed if you want to start fully topped up.

3.2. Goodsprings – First Steps into Town

Step outside into the bright Mojave. Turn slightly right and check the mailbox near Doc's house for any magazines. Then walk down the slope into town.

3.2.1. Meet the Locals in a Loop

  1. Walk toward the Prospector Saloon. Around the back you'll find Sunny Smiles practicing with her dog. Talk to her and agree to do the tutorial:
    • Target practice on bottles.
    • Clearing geckos at nearby wells.
    Loot each dead gecko for meat (cook it at campfires) and hides.
  2. Go inside the Prospector Saloon:
    • Talk to Trudy about what's going on in town and why Powder Gangers are a problem.
    • Listen when Joe Cobb comes in and threatens her – this sets up the Goodsprings vs. Powder Ganger conflict.
    • Behind the bar and on shelves, look for magazines such as Programmer's Digest, Salesman Weekly, and others. Take them all.
  3. Cross the street to Chet's General Store:
    • Talk to Chet, ask about supplies and town defense. This will matter for the upcoming fight.
    • Prioritize buying:
      • Varmint Rifle (if Sunny didn't give you one).
      • A cheap .357 revolver if you can afford it.
      • As many Stimpaks and Doctor's Bags as possible.
  4. Outside on the bench near the saloon, speak with Easy Pete. Ask about dynamite and about the old mine. This opens up more options for the fight.
Unmarked content Speak to every named local in Goodsprings and choose all dialogue options: a few tiny unmarked "favors" and flavor stories start here, especially around the saloon and the houses behind it.

3.3. Goodsprings Cemetery – First Snow Globe

  1. From the saloon, face north and walk up the dirt road that climbs the hill to the fenced Goodsprings Cemetery.
  2. On the way up, pick any Broc Flowers and Xander Roots you see; they are core ingredients for early healing powders.
  3. Enter the cemetery gate and walk toward the rows of fresh graves near the center. Look for a small stone base near the graves: sitting on it is the Goodsprings Snow Globe. Pick it up.
  4. Check the small maintenance shack for ammo boxes and a shovel (keep the shovel to dig graves and hidden caches later).

3.4. Ghost Town Gunfight – Siding with Goodsprings

You now choose whether to help Goodsprings or the Powder Gangers. For maximum content later, this guide assumes you help Goodsprings.

  1. Talk to Trudy, Sunny Smiles, Chet, and Easy Pete again:
    • Use Speech and Barter checks to convince them to help. If you care about not failing checks, save before each conversation.
    • From Easy Pete, use Speech to convince him to hand over dynamite.
  2. Once you're ready, tell Trudy to start the defense. Move to the main street between the saloon and Chet's store and get into cover.
  3. During the fight:
    • Let the townsfolk draw fire where possible.
    • Pick off Powder Gangers with your rifle or revolver from cover.
    • Afterward, loot every Powder Ganger: collect dynamite, armor, ammo, and chems.
Immersion After the shootout, sleep in one of the beds in the rock house opposite the saloon. Treat it as your Courier's last calm night before heading out alone into the Mojave.

4. Primm, Mojave Outpost & NCRCF Loop

First road trip and Powder Ganger web.

4.1. Goodsprings → Primm

  1. From Chet's store, face south-east and follow the cracked asphalt road out of town. Keep to the road, looting plants for food and healing ingredients.
  2. As you join the I-15, continue south until you see the roller coaster and neon: that's Primm.
  3. At the NCR checkpoint just outside, talk to the sergeant to understand the situation and get some NCR context.

4.2. Clearing Primm and Meeting the Sheriff Problem

  1. Carefully enter Primm – convicts patrol the streets. Use cover on the highway embankments if needed.
  2. Go to the Bison Steve Hotel (roller coaster attached) and clear it:
    • Move room by room, looting convicts for weapons (look for a 9mm SMG) and armor.
    • Check maintenance areas for the Varmint Rifle night-scope mod and more ammo.
  3. Head to the Vikki and Vance Casino:
    • Talk to Johnson Nash about your lost package to start the main quest line that follows the men who shot you.
    • Ask about the missing sheriff and what it means for the town.
    • Behind the bar and on shelves, pick up Sunset Sarsaparilla bottles and any star caps you see – always take star caps when you find them.
  4. Speak to the Primmslim robot in the casino. Look at his sheriff programming options but don't lock anything in yet.

4.3. Mojave Outpost – NCR Introductions

  1. Leave Primm southward along the road until you see the giant NCR statues: this is Mojave Outpost.
  2. Enter the main building with the bar:
    • Talk to Cass at the bar. Chat with her thoroughly so her future companion quest flags are set.
    • Talk to Ranger Jackson and accept his quest to clear ants blocking the road.
    • Check tables and shelves for magazines (Guns, Barter, Repair).
  3. Climb the stairs in the command building to find Major Knight:
    • Ask him to repair your armor for free once.
    • Mention Primm's sheriff problem to open one possible solution (NCR taking over law enforcement there).
  4. Go outside to the catwalk and speak with Ranger Ghost about the lands she's watching. This will mark more places on your map and foreshadow near-future story points.
  5. Complete Jackson's ant quest by following the road south to the marked area, clearing out the ants, and reporting back for XP and NCR reputation.

4.4. NCRCF – Powder Ganger Headquarters

  1. Fast travel back to Primm, then take the road north-east toward the fenced NCR Correctional Facility (NCRCF).
  2. Holster your weapon and talk your way in at the main gate if you're not hostile.
  3. Inside, your goal is to do Powder Ganger quests before you eventually turn on them:
    • Speak with Eddie about work. Accept any jobs he offers (hit jobs, small tasks, etc.).
    • Talk to other gangers in the cellblocks; some offer additional favors that function like unmarked quests (errands and deliveries).
  4. Loot carefully: treat the place as "owned" if you want to stay in their good graces for now. Focus on grabbing dynamite, ammo, and armor from corpses or unowned containers.
  5. Continue Eddie's questline until it naturally pauses or until a step would clearly set you on a collision course with the NCR main story. Then leave the gangers alone— you'll come back on tougher terms later.

5. Nipton, Novac & Repconn Test Site

Companions, dark lotteries, and rockets.

5.1. Primm → Nipton

  1. Leave Primm by the eastern bridge and follow the highway into the hills. Loot abandoned cars and small camps along the way for ammo and caps.
  2. As you draw closer, you'll see smoke and fire: that's Nipton. Walk in cautiously.
  3. Explore the town:
    • General Store: caps, ammo, random supplies – grab everything not nailed down.
    • Town Hall: carefully clear enemies inside and check desks, filing cabinets, and safes for loot and notes.
  4. Go to the town square: talk to the man ranting about winning the lottery, then to the Legion officer overseeing the slaughter.
  5. Afterward, wander behind Nipton up the hill to any camps: talk to any survivors or gangers there for extra unmarked mini-quests.

5.2. Nipton → Ranger Station Charlie → Novac

  1. Leave Nipton north along the main road. Off to the right, slightly off the road, step into Ranger Station Charlie and talk to whoever is in charge.
  2. Take note of the Ranger station status; this will be referenced in future NCR operations.
  3. Continue north until the giant T-Rex billboard and statue of Novac come into view.

5.3. Novac – Home Base & Boone

  1. Head to the Dino Dee-lite Motel office. Speak with Jeannie May and rent a room. This will be your early safehouse, with containers you can safely store gear in.
  2. Inside your room, loot dressers and tables, then sleep if you want to establish it mentally as your new "home".
  3. Walk to the base of the dinosaur and enter the Dino Bite Gift Shop:
    • Talk to Cliff Briscoe. Buy a Silenced .22 Pistol (handy later for stealth and GRA challenges).
  4. Climb the stairs up into the dinosaur's mouth at night to meet Boone. Talk to him thoroughly about his past and accept his quest, One for My Baby.
  5. Investigate the town for betrayal clues:
    • Search Jeannie May's house (use Lockpick or obtain her key), find the safe, and read any incriminating documents inside.
    • Talk to locals like Manny Vargas and others to learn more about Novac's history.
  6. Once you are satisfied you know the culprit, follow Boone's instructions: lure the betrayer in front of the dinosaur at night and signal Boone. This resolves his quest and allows him to join you as a companion.

5.4. Novac – Manny & Main Quest

Visit Manny Vargas (upstairs in the motel rooms) and talk about your attackers. He will provide the next lead in the main quest, pointing you toward New Vegas via specific routes.

5.5. Side Loop – Repconn Test Site "Come Fly With Me"

  1. From Novac, walk north-west across the open ground to the Repconn Test Site – a rocket museum with a tower.
  2. Clear the outside of feral ghouls carefully, then go inside.
  3. In the lobby, talk to the ghoul in charge to begin Come Fly With Me.
  4. Work your way through:
    • Office floors – check desks and small offices for a Science or Energy Weapons skill book on shelves or tables. Pick up every skill book you find, but don't read them yet if you don't have Comprehension.
    • Basement/reactor levels – you'll meet more ghoul factions and resolve their conflicts via combat or skill checks.
  5. Complete the rocket quest in whichever way best suits your Courier, using Speech, Science, or brute force as available.
Companions After finishing Boone's personal quest, you can recruit him as your first permanent companion. His presence shifts some Legion encounters and opens unique dialogue later.

6. Approach to New Vegas

188 Trading Post, implants & Freeside.

6.1. Novac → 188 Trading Post

  1. Leave Novac heading north-west, keeping to the main roads to avoid Cazador-infested high ground.
  2. Eventually you'll reach an overpass with traders: the 188 Trading Post.
  3. Talk to every merchant and passer-by. Look for Veronica, the woman in a Brotherhood of Steel scribe outfit. Speak with her about the Brotherhood and the world until she offers to travel with you.

6.2. 188 → New Vegas Medical Clinic – Implants

  1. From 188, walk north-east to the New Vegas Medical Clinic.
  2. Inside, talk to Doctor Usanagi. Tell her you're interested in implants.
  3. Buy implants in this priority order as caps allow:
    • Intelligence Implant – to reach 10 INT as soon as possible.
    • Endurance Implant – more health and more available implant slots.
    • Luck Implant – for better crits and skill bonuses.
    • Remaining SPECIAL implants in any order that suits your build.
    • Finally, the Sub-dermal Armor Implant (DT bonus) once you have surplus caps.

6.3. Freeside – Followers, Kings & Second Snow Globe

  1. From the clinic, head west toward Freeside and enter through the east gate.
  2. Once inside, walk to the Old Mormon Fort (Followers of the Apocalypse HQ). Go inside the main tent/building and look on the counters: there is a Snow Globe (Freeside/Mormon Fort) sitting there. Pick it up.
  3. Talk to Julie Farkas and the doctors about chems, addicts, and food/water. Accept any quests or offers to help.
  4. Wander the Freeside streets:
    • Visit Mick & Ralph's – ask about their "real" inventory and check for useful gear and magazines.
    • Head into the School of Impersonation and talk to The King to begin the GI Blues questline.
    • Visit the Atomic Wrangler and speak with the Garretts for work.
    • Talk to Old Ben and various Freeside drifters for tiny unmarked favors and leads.

7. The Strip & Snow Globes

Vault 21, Lucky 38 & factions.

7.1. Entering the Strip

  1. Once you have enough caps (or the right skill check to bypass security), head through the Strip gate.
  2. Take a moment to familiarize yourself with the three main Strip sections.

7.2. Snow Globe – Vault 21 (The Strip)

  1. Walk along the Strip until you spot the entrance to the Vault 21 Hotel.
  2. Go in and look at the front reception desk: the Strip/Vault 21 Snow Globe rests there. Pick it up.

7.3. Lucky 38 & Snow Globe Turn-Ins

  1. Shortly after entering, a Securitron will invite you to the Lucky 38. Accept and enter.
  2. After your first meeting in the penthouse, explore the cocktail lounge. Look on the bar counter for another Snow Globe (Lucky 38). Take it.
  3. Look for the Securitron named Jane in the presidential suite. Speak with her about the snow globes and sell them to her for caps when you like. She will display them in a case, but the achievement is already locked in when you collected them.

7.4. Factions on the Strip

On The Strip, you connect with each major endgame faction:

  • Mr. House – through further conversations in the Lucky 38.
  • NCR – via the NCR Embassy and Ambassador Crocker.
  • Yes Man – through an encounter in The Tops casino.
  • Caesar's Legion – by invitations and later visits to The Fort.
Important Progress each faction's main questline slowly and evenly. Any time you're asked to destroy or permanently cripple another major faction, stop there and go do other quests first. You'll pick one path for the final battle only after everything else (and all DLC) is complete.

8. Region Sweeps – Clearing the Mojave

Outer Vegas, south, north & Hoover Dam.

With the Strip unlocked and your implants started, you can begin systematically clearing the Mojave. Use these loops so you don't miss quest hubs and locations.

8.1. Outer Vegas Loop – McCarran, Aerotech & Vault 3

  1. From The Strip or Freeside, head south to Camp McCarran:
    • Enter the main terminal and talk to Colonel Hsu, Dr. Hildern, and Lieutenant Boyd. Accept their investigations and vault assignments.
    • Explore the offices and lab; loot desks for magazines and check private quarters for unique armor pieces and weapons.
    • Talk to any named troopers; some quietly ask for help, triggering unmarked or small side quests.
  2. Move east toward Aerotech Office Park and Crimson Caravan Company:
    • At Aerotech, talk to residents and refugees about missing people and debts. Note any leads.
    • At Crimson Caravan, talk to the company head and staff for caravan-related quests and to set up future content with Cass.
  3. Head south-west to Vault 3, Fiend territory:
    • Work with McCarran's Fiend-related quests if you have them, then infiltrate or storm Vault 3.
    • Inside, loot every dorm and office. Fiends stockpile chems, ammo, and some rare weapons.
    • Check overseer and water treatment areas for skill books and unique gear.

8.2. Southern Loop – Searchlight, Forlorn Hope, Nelson & Cottonwood

  1. Travel from Novac or The Strip toward Camp Searchlight:
    • Equip radiation protection (suits, Rad-X, RadAway) before entering the irradiated town.
    • Talk to NCR at the church and explore ruined houses, looking for dogtags, logs, and supplies.
  2. Continue east to Camp Forlorn Hope:
    • Speak to every officer; you'll pick up quests that involve retrieving supplies, fixing morale, and uncovering corruption.
  3. Move on to Nelson and Cottonwood Cove:
    • Decide how your Courier deals with Legion forces here; there are multiple solutions for liberating or supporting these locations.
    • Remember that Cottonwood Cove later serves as your ferry to The Fort.

8.3. Northern Loop – Nellis, Jacobstown, Black Mountain & Vaults

  1. Head north-east from Vegas to the Nellis Air Force Base:
    • Sprint between explosions as instructed to survive the artillery field.
    • Once inside, complete the Boomers' multi-step quest line: fixing generators, visiting their museum, and more.
    • In the Boomer Museum, check the displays and counters for the Nellis Snow Globe and pick it up.
  2. Travel north-west through frosty terrain to Jacobstown:
    • Inside the lodge, look at the reception area or front desk to find the Jacobstown Snow Globe.
    • Talk with Marcus and the Nightkin residents for super mutant storylines and quests (some marked, some not).
  3. Make your way to Black Mountain:
    • Follow the winding path up and clear the mutant radio outpost.
    • Search the broadcast building and nearby shacks for unique weapons and a skill book.
  4. Explore the central-northern vaults:
    • Vault 22: explore hydroponics and labs, reading logs about the plant experiment.
    • Vault 11: carefully explore every level and read terminals to understand the dark story; there are alternate endings and hidden rooms.
    • Vault 34: a tougher, irradiated vault with powerful weapons but dangerous enemies.

8.4. Hoover Dam & Visitor Center – Final Base-Game Snow Globe

  1. As part of NCR or main story progress, visit Hoover Dam.
  2. Enter the Hoover Dam Visitor Center. On a desk in the reception area, locate the Hoover Dam Snow Globe and pick it up.

With Goodsprings, Freeside, Strip/Vault 21, Lucky 38, Nellis, Jacobstown, and Hoover Dam globes, you complete the base-game snow globe collection.

9. Companions & Unmarked Stories

Getting "the whole gang" together.

For achievements and story flavor, you want to recruit each companion at least once and explore their personal arcs, many of which are technically unmarked.

9.1. Companion Locations

  • Boone – In the mouth of the dinosaur in Novac (after his "One for My Baby" quest).
  • Veronica – Wandering at the 188 Trading Post.
  • Cass – Drinking at the bar in the Mojave Outpost.
  • Raul – Imprisoned at Black Mountain; free him there.
  • Lily – In Jacobstown, inside the lodge.
  • Arcade Gannon – At the Old Mormon Fort in Freeside.
  • Rex – The King's cyber-dog in Freeside.
  • ED-E – Deactivated eyebot in the back of Johnson Nash's store in Primm.

9.2. Personal & Unmarked Companion Quests

Many companion stories advance by bringing them to specific locations and talking to them after major events:

  • Veronica: Visit Brotherhood-related locations and discuss her views on the order.
  • Raul: Bring him to Black Mountain and later Jacobstown and talk about his identity.
  • Cass: Involves Crimson Caravan and the Wrangler, plus a revenge plot with branching resolutions.
  • Arcade: Encourage or discourage his idealism while visiting NCR and science hubs.
  • Rex: A quest related to replacing his brain – visit specific clinics and locations, then choose his new brain source.
Routine Whenever you finish a significant quest arc or visit a major new location, talk to your active companion. If they have something to say, follow it – that's usually the next step in their personal story.

10. Maxing Skills & SPECIAL

Implants, books & long-term progression.

10.1. When to Read Skill Books

Throughout the Mojave and all DLCs, you'll find permanent skill books. To maximize their value:

  • Do not read any skill books until you have the Comprehension perk (ideally taken at Level 6).
  • After that, each book gives +4 to a skill instead of +2.

Places to hunt for skill books:

  • Bookcases in vaults, homes, and offices.
  • Desks and shelves in NCR bases and Strip casinos.
  • Various DLC safes, laboratories, and hidden caches.

10.2. Achieving 100 in All Skills

With 10 Intelligence, the Educated and Comprehension perks, plus all DLC raising the level cap to 50, you can reach 100 in every skill.

  • Keep your early level-up points focused on Speech, Repair, Guns, Lockpick, and Science for checks and utility.
  • As you start reading skill books mid-game, top off under-leveled skills using their +4 bonuses.

10.3. SPECIAL Maxing

To push SPECIAL as high as the game reasonably allows:

  • Buy all SPECIAL implants from Dr. Usanagi in the medical clinic, limited by your Endurance.
  • Complete Old World Blues; accept all organ upgrades (Spine, Heart, Brain) for permanent stat buffs.
  • Finish Lonesome Road and choose the SPECIAL bonus option at the end, adding +1 to the stat that best rounds out your build.
Checklist
  • Educated taken by Level 4.
  • Comprehension taken by Level 6.
  • All SPECIAL implants purchased.
  • All organ upgrades completed in Old World Blues.
  • Lonesome Road SPECIAL bonus chosen.

11. DLCs – Order & Key Priorities

Dead Money, Honest Hearts, Old World Blues, Lonesome Road.

Tackle DLCs after you reach roughly level 20–25 and before the final Hoover Dam battle. This keeps challenge fair and still lets you bring their rewards into the finale.

11.1. Dead Money – Sierra Madre

  • How to start: Listen to the Sierra Madre broadcast which appears near the Mojave Drive-In in the south-east desert.
  • The DLC strips you of your gear when you arrive, so:
    • Come with high Sneak, Unarmed/Melee, and plenty of patience.
    • Scavenge everything: chips, food, and stimpaks are scarce but vital.
  • Collect Sierra Madre chips obsessively; there is an achievement tied to amassing them.
  • Explore every villa rooftop and alley for holotapes and codes; many companions' arcs play out through these optional finds.
  • At the vault finale, use manual saves to experience both major ending variations if you want to see all possible outcomes.

11.2. Honest Hearts – Zion

  • How to start: Travel to the Northern Passage north of New Vegas and speak with the caravan there.
  • Once in Zion, clear every side canyon and cave:
    • Look for survivalist caches with powerful loot and journals.
    • Collect new recipes and high-Tier survival gear.
  • The main choice eventually falls between Daniel and Joshua Graham. Make a manual save before committing so you can see both endings if you wish.

11.3. Old World Blues – Big MT

  • How to start: Wait for the Big MT/OWB broadcast and visit the Mojave Drive-In at night.
  • Big MT is a playground for weird science:
    • Explore every marked facility – labs, test chambers, dig sites. Many hide skill books, unique energy weapons, and notes.
    • Progress the main story until you unlock The Sink, your new house. Activate and upgrade all of its robot appliances for utilities and an achievement.
  • When offered organ replacements, always choose the upgraded options for permanent bonuses to health, DR, and other stats.

11.4. Lonesome Road – The Divide

  • How to start: Follow the radio and quest that directs you to the Canyon Wreckage near Primm.
  • This DLC is linear and combat-heavy:
    • Bring your best weapons and armor.
    • Stock ammo, stimpaks, and repair kits.
  • Keep an eye out for ED-E logs and upgrades; finding them all grants an achievement.
  • Look around for glowing warheads that you can detonate. There is a challenge for destroying all of them; check side tunnels and dead-ends carefully.
  • At the climax, your choices alter the fate of The Divide and reward you with a SPECIAL +1 bonus – choose the stat that best balances your build.

12. Gun Runners’ Arsenal – Folding Challenges In

Challenges without grinding.

Gun Runners' Arsenal (GRA) adds special weapon challenges. You only need a subset of them for the achievements, so weave the easiest ones into your normal play.

12.1. General Tips

  • Frequently check Data → Misc → Challenges on your Pip-Boy, especially the GRA tab.
  • When you can, buy GRA-marked versions of common weapons and use them instead of the vanilla counterparts to rack up damage for the "10,000 damage" achievements.

12.2. Easy One-Star Challenges

Pick three of these to clear the one-star requirement:

  • A Slave Obeys – If you choose not to side with Mr. House, kill him using a 9 Iron or Nephi's Golf Driver. Remember to bring the club before meeting him in his control room.
  • Ne Ne Ne Ne… – Cripple five enemies' right arms with any shotgun. Use VATS and target the right arm on lightly armored enemies.
  • Overkill – Kill a dog or coyote with a Fat Man or Fat Mine. Lure a weak target somewhere safe and fire a mini-nuke.

12.3. Two- and Three-Star Example Challenges

Good candidates that align with normal combat:

  • White Line Nightmare – Kill Fiends, Vipers, and Jackals with specific weapons. Focus on this when clearing Fiend territories like around Vault 3.
  • Vault 13's Revenge – Kill super mutants with specified weapon types. Target Black Mountain and areas around Jacobstown while this is active.
  • Against All Tyrants (3-star) – Kill named Legion members with certain weapons. Save it for late game if you decide to oppose the Legion.
  • Other combat-focused challenges like Deathclaw Pro Hunter or Man-Machine Interface, depending on which enemies you are already fighting often.

12.4. Damage Achievements

  • GRA Damage with Unique Weapons – Pick a favorite unique gun or melee weapon and use it for most mid-late fights.
  • GRA Damage with GRA Weapons – Similarly, once you purchase GRA guns, prioritize them over vanilla versions until the damage challenge completes.

13. Final Main Quest Push & Multi-Ending Strategy

One save, all endgames.

13.1. Before Hoover Dam

Before committing to the final battle, ensure you have:

  • Completed all side quests you care about (check Pip-Boy quest list).
  • Finished all four story DLCs.
  • Collected all base-game snow globes and turned them in if you want caps.
  • Discovered at least 125 locations (base game + DLCs).
  • Maxed or nearly maxed all skills, and purchased all desired implants.

13.2. Creating Your "Master Save"

  1. Progress the main story with the faction you lean toward, up until the point where the game clearly warns you that you're committing to a final path at Hoover Dam.
  2. Create a new, clearly labeled hard save here, such as: Pre-Hoover Dam – Final Choice.
  3. This save is your gateway to all main-ending achievements.

13.3. Running Through Each Ending

  1. From your master save, choose one faction (e.g., NCR) and proceed through the final missions to victory. Watch the ending slides and credits.
  2. Once you've returned to the main menu, reload the master save and align with a different faction (e.g., Mr. House or Yes Man), making different choices at the key moments.
  3. Repeat this process for all factions whose endings you want to see and whose achievements you still need.
Hardcore Mode As long as you never disabled Hardcore Mode during this save and reached the credits once, the Hardcore achievement is safe. You don't need to re-run the whole story again.

14. Immersive Playstyle Tips

Staying in character while seeing everything.

14.1. Conversation First

  • Talk to every named NPC fully and exhaust peaceful dialogue options before pulling a trigger.
  • Always ask "Tell me about this place" and "Any work available?" – many side and unmarked quests hide behind those lines.

14.2. Reading the World

  • Read every note, holotape, and terminal you find. Many unmarked objectives and stash hints only live in text.
  • When characters mention places or people, mark them mentally or on paper if you don't yet have a map marker.

14.3. Checking Items as You Go

As a quick mental checklist when you enter any new interior location:

  • Walk the perimeter and note any closed doors and locked containers.
  • Check every bookshelf for skill books.
  • Check bar counters, desks, and reception areas for snow globes or unique items.
  • Look for owned vs unowned containers if you're minding faction reputation.

14.4. Saving Smart

  • Keep at least three rolling manual saves: one recent, one from earlier in the current questline, and one "clean" save from before major faction choices.
  • Save before big Speech checks, robberies, or any event that feels like it might have long-term consequences for factions or companions.

14.5. Walking the Roads

When the guide tells you to head somewhere, try actually walking there along the roads instead of fast traveling the first time. You'll:

  • Discover more map locations.
  • Encounter random events and traveling NPCs with tiny stories.
  • Make the Mojave feel like a single connected space instead of a menu of fast travel points.
Use this page as your wasteland field manual: follow the route, mark off checklists, and let your Courier make the final calls when choices appear.
